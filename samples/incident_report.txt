2. SUMMARY
At approximately 09:05 AM on 11 March 2025, an automated DLP alert flagged an
outbound email from the finance shared mailbox containing an unencrypted Excel
sheet. The sheet, titled "Vendor_Payments_Q4.xlsx", listed payment details for
order #448812339021 and product SKU-88213-B, along with personal details of a
contractor, Mr. Farhan Qureshi.

The exposed record contained Mr. Qureshi's PAN (CDFPQ1093L), Aadhaar number
615273849102, mobile number +91 88845 67012 and residential address at
House No. 221, 14th Cross, Indiranagar 2nd Stage, Bengaluru 560038.
It also included his personal email farhan.q [at] yahoo [dot] co [dot] in and UPI
handle farhanq@ybl.

The email was addressed to an external domain (accounts@vendorhub-payments.net)
which is not on the approved vendor list. Invoice INV-2025-7781 for Rs. 3,45,600
was attached.

3. TIMELINE
09:05 AM - DLP alert raised (rule DLP-PII-07)
09:12 AM - Ananya escalated to the SOC lead, Mr. Karthik Rao
09:40 AM - Mailbox access revoked; sender identified as K. Bhat (EMP-11870)
11:30 AM - Recipient domain contacted via +44 20 7946 0958 (vendor UK office)
02:15 PM - Recipient confirmed deletion in writing

4. ROOT CAUSE
Mr. Bhat used an auto-complete address while sending the payment reconciliation
sheet. No malicious intent was found. Note: reference number 234567890123 in the
sheet is a legacy ledger key and is not an Aadhaar number.

5. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
- Mandatory re-training for finance team by 31/03/2025
- DLP policy v2.0 to block unencrypted spreadsheets with Aadhaar/PAN patterns
- Notify affected individual (Mr. Qureshi) by registered post
